# PHASE 30 — RESEARCH & FACULTY DEVELOPMENT

## Pre-Requisite
Phases 01–06 complete (staff + faculty profiles with ORCID/Scopus ids and `api_score` fields, workflow engine, communication core), Phase 12 (fees for consultancy receipts), Phase 22 (procurement for project purchases), Phase 23 (`grant_ledgers` for funded projects), Phase 25 (events for FDPs/conferences), Phase 27 (committee stub) — Phase 33 replaces the committee stub. Phase 41 supplies live adapters for Scopus/Crossref/ORCID; mocks are used until then.

## Objective
The research office and faculty development record system: **publications** (journal/conference/book/chapter with indexing, impact factor, author positions, DOI de-duplication, Crossref/Scopus/ORCID imports, verification), **patents**, **funded research projects** (application → sanction → ongoing → completed/closed) with agency budget heads, purchase/expense requests routed through procurement and grant ledgers, utilisation and reports, **consultancy** with institution share and receipts, **ethics committees** (IEC/IAEC/IBSC) approvals, **seed grants, publication incentives, travel grants, patent support** via workflow, **FDP & faculty development** records (attended/organised/resource person, hours), **awards**, **MoUs & collaborations** with activity tracking and expiry, **incubation/start-ups** register, and **API/CAS score computation** feeding Phase 06 appraisals/CAS — with self (my research profile) vs HOD (department) vs research cell (all) views, and NAAC 3.x / NIRF RP metrics.

## Feature keys
`research.publications`, `research.patents`, `research.projects_grants` (depends `accounting.grants_ledger`), `research.consultancy`, `research.fdp`, `research.mous_collaborations` (on, standard); `research.ethics_committee` (off, professional; depends `governance.committees`), `research.seed_grants_incentives` (off, professional; depends `workflow.engine`), `research.incubation` (off, professional), `research.api_score` (off, professional; depends `research.publications`); `integrations.scopus_crossref_orcid`, `integrations.plagiarism` (adapters).

## 30.6 Services
- `publication_service.py` (`research.publications`)
  1. Records by faculty/scholars with authors tagged to staff/scholars (co-authors within the institution share one row — `authors[].staff_id`; each tagged author sees it as own); DOI normalisation and duplicate policy (`block` → 409 with the existing row and a "claim co-authorship" action; `warn`); indexing and impact factor from imports; evidence upload.
  2. Imports: Crossref by DOI/ORCID, Scopus by author id (adapter `BibliographicProvider`, mock returns fixtures), BibTeX/CSV files → preview (matches to existing by DOI/title similarity, duplicates, new) → apply; nightly sync for faculty with ORCID/Scopus ids when opted in; citations refresh (Scopus) when live.
  3. Verification workflow-lite: HOD first-level (`research:verify` at department) → research cell final (`is_verified`, `verified_by`); rejected with remarks; verified rows locked for authors (change requests via research cell); counts by indexing feed NAAC 3.3.x and NIRF PU/QP.
- `patent_service.py` (`research.patents`): filed → published → granted → commercialised with dates, co-inventors, evidence; reminders for renewal (setting); NAAC 3.4.x.
- `project_service.py` (`research.projects_grants`)
  1. Lifecycle: `applied` (proposal record, agency, scheme) → `sanctioned` (sanction letter, sanctioned amount, budget heads; workflow `research.project_sanction`: research cell verifies → principal acknowledges) → creates Phase 23 `grant_ledgers` (event `research.project_sanctioned` consumed by Phase 23; `grant_ledger_id` back-linked) → `ongoing` (receipts recorded in the ledger) → `completed` (final report) → `closed` (UC submitted, unspent refunded).
  2. Expenses: PI raises `ProjectExpenseRequest` → `via=indent` → Phase 22 indent with `budget_head_id` = ledger head mapping (procurement rules apply) or `via=direct` → Phase 23 grant expense (travel/contingency) with attachments; head-wise available from the ledger; manpower (project staff/JRF) salaries via Phase 06 payroll `source_type='research_project'` when configured; reports due schedule (agency periodicity) with reminders; project staff list.
  3. Reports: statement of expenditure/UC via Phase 23, progress reports uploaded per period, closure checklist (assets transferred to institution register Phase 22 on closure).
- `consultancy_service.py` (`research.consultancy`): agreements, `institution_share_paise` auto from `consultancy_share_pct` (override with approval), receipts through Phase 12 invoice (`payer_type='company'`, `source_type='consultancy'`) → institution share to income, faculty share to payroll (`payroll.earning_requested`, TDS handled by payroll) or direct payment record; NAAC 3.5.x metrics.
- `ethics_service.py` (`research.ethics_committee`): committees (IEC/IAEC/IBSC) bound to Phase 33 committees with registration numbers and members; applications (protocol documents, category: exempt/expedited/full review) → workflow `research.ethics_review` (member reviews → chair decides) → `approval_no`, validity, conditions, amendments/renewals, adverse event reporting; approvals linkable to projects/PhD (Phase 31) and required before project start when `ethics_required_for_categories` matches.
- `incentive_service.py` (`research.seed_grants_incentives`): requests by faculty (seed grant with proposal + budget, publication incentive linked to a verified publication with slab computation by indexing/author position, travel grant linked to an accepted paper/conference with caps, patent filing support) → workflow `research.incentive` (hod → research cell → dean/principal by amount) → approved → disbursement (Phase 23 expense record / payroll earning line) → `disbursed`; one incentive per publication (UNIQUE via linked entity check); annual caps per faculty; register for NAAC 3.1.x/6.3.x evidence.
- `fdp_service.py` (`research.fdp`): records per staff (attended/organised/resource person; hours; certificates; link to Phase 25 events when organised in-house — auto-create records for registered staff on event completion); annual FDP hours compliance per faculty (setting target) for appraisal (Phase 06 PBAS auto-fill); NAAC 6.3.3/6.3.4 counts.
- `award_service.py`: awards/recognitions with level and evidence; verification; feeds NAAC 3.x/6.x and profile.
- `mou_service.py` (`research.mous_collaborations`): MoUs with partner type, validity, documents, coordinator, activities plan; workflow `research.mou` before signing (research cell → legal → principal); collaborations under MoUs (joint research, exchange, joint degree, internships → Phase 28 companies link) with participants and outcomes; expiry alerts (`mou_expiry_alert_days`), activity count per year (NAAC 3.7.x requires functional MoUs with activities); renewal creates a new version.
- `incubation_service.py` (`research.incubation`): start-ups register (founders: students/alumni/faculty tags), stage progression, funding, mentors, space/asset allocation (Phase 25 bookings), events; NAAC 3.x / NIRF innovation counts.
- `api_score_service.py` (`research.api_score`): compute per faculty for a period using the configured scheme tables (UGC CAS 2018 categories: teaching/learning (from Phase 06 workload/feedback), co-curricular/extension (from Phase 27/25), research publications/projects/consultancy/patents/FDP/awards with indexing/author-position weights and caps) → breakdown stored on `faculty_profiles.api_score/api_updated_at` + a JSON snapshot in `institution_settings['research.api_snapshots']` keyed by staff/period; consumed by Phase 06 appraisals/CAS (auto-fill and eligibility); department ranking report.
- `stats_service.py`: publications per teacher (verified only), by indexing, grants amount received per year, consultancy revenue, patents, FDP participation, MoUs with activities, start-ups; NAAC 3.x and NIRF RP (PU, QP, IPR, FPPP) values published to Phase 32 (`research.*` metrics) with evidence suggestions (`evidence_documents` auto-tag).
