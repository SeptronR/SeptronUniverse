# PHASE 33 — GOVERNANCE: COMMITTEES, MEETINGS, LEGAL & e-FILE

## Pre-Requisite
Phases 01–06 complete (staff, roles/scopes incl. `committee_secretary` @committee, workflow engine, communication core, documents from Phase 26 for minutes/orders PDFs, calendar from Phase 25 for meeting scheduling). Phases 16, 27, 30, 31, 32 already created committee rows through the stub CRUD (`governance:committees`) and cite `resolutions` — this phase replaces the stub with the full module (same tables, no data migration) and wires `regulations.approval_resolution_id`, `result_processing_runs.approval_resolution_id`, `degrees_awarded.approval_resolution_id`, `policies.approved_resolution_id`.

## Objective
Institutional governance as records: **committees** (statutory and custom: BoS, Academic Council, Executive Council/Senate, Finance, IQAC, Anti-ragging, ICC, Grievance, Research Board, Purchase, Discipline, Ethics) with members (internal/external/student), tenure and quorum rules; **meetings** (schedule, agenda with documents, notices, attendance, quorum check, minutes drafting/approval, resolutions numbered per committee, action-taken reports and follow-ups); **resolution linkage** to regulations, result approvals, degree awards, policies and other entities; **legal case register** (courts, hearings, counsel, documents, outcomes) with hearing reminders; **RTI** requests (receipt → PIO assignment → response within 30 days, fees, first appeals, annual return); **statutory/AG audit paras** register linked to Phase 32 audit registers; **e-File** (file numbers, movement with notings, forwarding/return/approve, holders, SLA/pendency, closure); **policy repository** (versions, approval resolution, effective dates, acknowledgements) — with self (my committees/meetings/files) vs secretary (own committees) vs registrar/office (all) views.

## Feature keys
`governance.committees`, `governance.minutes`, `governance.policies` (on, standard); `governance.legal`, `governance.rti`, `governance.efile` (off, professional); `governance.audit_paras` (off, professional; depends `accreditation.audit_registers`).

## 33.6 Services
- `committee_service.py` (`governance.committees`): create from templates or custom; members with tenure (`from_on/to_on`), external members (no login required; optional external account for document access), student members; chair/secretary; validity and reconstitution (new tenure keeps history); quorum rule (`fraction|count`); committee page with meetings/resolutions/ATR status; statutory committee coverage check (anti-ragging, ICC, grievance mandatory per pack) → Phase 32 evidence (7.1.x) and Phase 27 routing uses these committees; migrate stub-created rows unchanged.
- `meeting_service.py` (`governance.minutes`)
  1. Schedule (meeting number auto per committee/year), agenda items with documents and linked entities (e.g. regulation draft, result run, degree batch, policy draft, audit para) → notice (Phase 26 template) sent to members via channels ≥ `notice_days` before (or flagged as short notice), calendar entries (Phase 25), reminders; agenda additions until freeze; circulation of pre-read documents with signed links.
  2. Held: attendance marking (members + invitees), quorum evaluation against `quorum_rule` (not met → meeting recorded as adjourned/`cancelled` or continues with a flag per setting); minutes drafting (rich text or upload) with resolutions per agenda item → `minutes_draft` → workflow `governance.minutes_approval` (secretary → chair) → `minutes_approved` → minutes PDF (Phase 26) and circulation; corrigenda create a new minutes version with an approval record; approved minutes immutable.
  3. Resolutions: numbered from `resolution:{committee_code}:{yyyy}` (e.g. `AC/2026/14`), status `passed|deferred|rejected`, action owner + due date, `linked_entity` (type/id) — linking API used by Phase 05 (regulation approval), Phase 15 (result approval), Phase 18 (degree awards), Phase 33 policies, Phase 22 purchase committee decisions, Phase 27 discipline; resolution extract PDF (certified) on request; `implemented` when ATR accepted.
  4. Action-taken reports: owner submits before `due_on` (reminders), secretary accepts/returns; ATR consolidated for the next meeting agenda automatically (“ATR on previous minutes”); pending-ATR dashboard.
  5. BoS specifics: agenda items auto-suggested from Phase 05 (draft regulations/courses awaiting approval) and Phase 17 CQI actions; Academic Council items from BoS resolutions; Finance Committee items from Phase 23 budgets; IQAC meetings from Phase 32 gaps/consistency alerts (agenda suggestions API).
- `legal_service.py` (`governance.legal`): case register (`case_no` unique; court, type, parties, counsel with fees tracked as Phase 23 expenses optional), hearings log with next-hearing reminders (`hearing_reminder_days`), documents (secure, `legal:manage` only), outcomes/judgements, appeals as linked cases, status; institution-as-petitioner/respondent tagging; confidentiality: only legal officer/principal/registrar; summary counts to Phase 32 (7.x) and Phase 36 annual report.
- `rti_service.py` (`governance.rti`): receipt (`request_no`, applicant details, BPL fee waiver), `due_on = received_on + response_days` (transfer under section 6(3) adjusts), fee via Phase 12 invoice (`source_type='rti_fee'`) or recorded cash/IPO, PIO assignment and internal consultations (e-file link for gathering information from departments), response with document (Phase 26 template) and additional fee for copies, first appeal to the appellate authority within `appeal_days` with decision, second appeal (external) note, status; due-date reminders; **quarterly/annual RTI return** export (counts, fees, disposals) for Phase 36; applicant PII masked outside PIO.
- `audit_para_service.py` (`governance.audit_paras`): paras under a Phase 32 `audit_registers` row (financial statutory/AG audits): description, amount, owner (department/finance), replies with attachments, status `open → replied → settled|dropped`, compliance report per audit, ageing dashboard, pending paras to the Finance Committee agenda (suggestion), NAAC 6.4.x evidence.
- `efile_service.py` (`governance.efile`)
  1. Create file (`file_no` from sequence per year/category), subject, category, priority, attachments, initial noting; movement model: `forward` to a user or role (resolved to a user at the receiving end by role@scope), `return`, `approve`, `reject` — each with noting (append-only notings sheet) and attachments; `current_holder_user_id`; pendency = time with the current holder; SLA (`pendency_sla_days`) reminders and escalation to the holder's reporting head (Phase 06 hierarchy) ; delegation while on leave (workflow delegation rules from Phase 04 reused); close with summary; reopen by admin.
  2. Inbox (files with me), outbox (files I sent), tracked files (created by me); search by file no/subject; audit trail of every movement (immutable); PDF of notings sheet; linking to entities (RTI request, purchase file, legal case, policy draft); optional `efile.approval` workflow for structured routes (e.g. purchase file: indent → finance → principal).
- `policy_service.py` (`governance.policies`): repository of institutional policies (categories: academic, examination, IT, POSH, anti-ragging, research ethics, data protection, HR, finance, hostel), versions with effective dates, approval via workflow `governance.policy_approval` ending in a council resolution link (when `governance.minutes`), publication to staff/students with **acknowledgement** tracking for required categories (Phase 24 announcement with ack), supersession chains, website publication flag (Phase 40), search; policy summary feeds Phase 24 AI assistant tools and Phase 32 evidence (7.1.x, 6.x).
- `stats_service.py`: meetings per committee per year, quorum compliance, resolutions implemented %, ATR pendency, legal cases by status, RTI disposal within time %, audit paras settled %, e-file pendency, policy acknowledgement %; to Phase 32/35/36.
