# PHASE 39 — PORTALS, PWA, NOTIFICATION CENTRE & GUIDED HELP

## Pre-Requisite
Phases 01–38 complete (every module already ships its self-service pages under the portal prefixes — `/student/*` (Phases 07–18, 19, 20, 25, 27, 28), `/parent/*` (07, 11, 12, 20, 27), `/faculty/*` (06, 09–13, 14, 15, 27, 30), `/alumni/*` (29), `/warden/*` (20), `/evaluator/*` (15), `/scholar/*` (31), `/apply/*` (08), `/recruiter/*` (28), `/driver` (21); Phase 01 shipped `PortalLayout`, `PortalRoute`, the PWA skeleton (`public/manifest.json`, `sw.js`), the notification-bell placeholder and help/feedback endpoints; Phase 04 shipped notifications, preferences, device tokens and the push channel; Phase 35 provides the portal Home widgets via `GET /dashboard`; Phase 38 provides help articles, announcements and feedback triage). Phase 40 reuses the branding/manifest service for the public website; Phase 42 relies on the universal-link association files served here.

## Objective
Turn the per-module portal pages into **coherent, branded, installable portals** for each non-admin persona — student, parent, faculty (incl. class advisor/mentor/HOD self-service), alumni, hostel warden/gate staff, external evaluator, research scholar, applicant, recruiter, driver — with a shared portal shell (institution branding, term chip, ward switcher, approvals inbox, notifications, profile, language), consolidated cross-module self-service pages (**My requests**, **My documents**, **My payments**, **My approvals**, **My calendar**, **My timeline**), portal information architecture aligned with `docs/User_Flows.md` and `docs/Mobile_App_UI_Structure.md`, a **PWA** per institution (dynamic manifest with branding, service worker with app-shell precache, stale-while-revalidate for read APIs, offline fallback, background sync for feedback/notification acks, install prompt, update toast, screenshot-safe pages), **web push** (VAPID; `device_tokens(provider='webpush')`; topics; per-category opt-in), the full **notification centre** (inbox with categories/filters/deep links, mark read/all, digest & quiet hours preferences, announcement acknowledgements), **guided tours** per portal/route/role with completion tracking, **contextual help** and feedback on every page, web **i18n** (`i18n.web`: English + Hindi packs for portal strings, language switch), accessibility (WCAG AA, axe in Playwright), print stylesheets, and deep-link parity with mobile (`/verify/:token`, `/apply/:cycle`, `/.well-known/*` association files).

## Feature keys
`portals.student`, `portals.parent`, `portals.faculty` (on, starter), `portals.pwa_push` (on, starter), `portals.alumni` (on, standard; depends `alumni.core`), `portals.evaluator` (on, standard; depends `exams.external_examiners`), `portals.warden` (off, standard; depends `hostel.core`), `portals.scholar` (off, professional; depends `phd.core`), `portals.recruiter` (off, professional; depends `placement.recruiter_portal`), `platform.guided_tours`, `platform.help_center`, `platform.in_app_feedback` (Phase 01, on), `i18n.web` (off, standard), `comms.preferences`/`comms.push` (Phase 04). Applicant (`admissions.online_application`) and driver (`transport.core`) portals are gated by their module keys.

## Visibility rules
Portals are **self/ownership only** — every portal page calls `/…/me` shortcuts or list endpoints whose resolver yields self (student, alumni, scholar, applicant, driver), wards (parent, gated by `students.guardian_portal_consent`), ownership (faculty: own offerings/mentees/advisees/duties/bundles; warden: own hostels; evaluator: assigned bundles with dummy numbers only; recruiter: own drives/consented applicant fields); no ScopeTabs are rendered in portals (`meta.visibility.scopes` is ignored, `self`/`own` forced client-side and server-side). Multi-role users (faculty who is also HOD) get the faculty portal plus an **"Admin view"** switch to the admin shell (Phase 02 `PORTAL_HOME` + role switch); approvals inbox in the faculty portal shows only steps assigned to the user. Notifications, preferences, device tokens, tours state and feedback are self only. Portal features disabled → route shows "portal not enabled" and the login redirect falls back to the next available home.
