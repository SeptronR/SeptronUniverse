# PHASE 31 — PhD & RESEARCH-SCHOLAR MANAGEMENT

## Pre-Requisite
Phases 01–18 complete (programmes with `level='phd'`, admissions incl. entrance/interview, students/enrolments, course registration and results for coursework, fees, exam engine for coursework exams, degrees engine for the PhD award), Phase 23 (fellowship disbursement postings), Phase 26 (documents: registration letters, provisional PhD certificate), Phase 30 (publications tagging, ethics approvals, guide research profiles). Phase 33 replaces the committee stub; Phase 41 supplies live plagiarism (DrillBit/Urkund/Turnitin) and Shodhganga adapters — mocks until then.

## Objective
The UGC PhD Regulations 2022 lifecycle as a workflow: scholar record on admission with **guide/co-guide allocation** under load limits (by cadre from `faculty_profiles.max_scholars`), mode (full/part-time) and fellowship type; **coursework** (credit requirement incl. research methodology, exemptions) tracked through Phase 09/15; **doctoral committee** (DC/RAC) constitution and periodic meetings with minutes/recommendations; **half-yearly progress reports** (scholar → guide → DC rating; two unsatisfactory → cancellation review); **fellowships** (JRF/SRF/institution/project) with monthly disbursements, contingency, HRA, and payroll/accounts integration; **scholar requests** (extension, change of guide, mode change, leave, coursework exemption) via workflow; **pre-synopsis/synopsis/thesis submissions** with plagiarism check and AI-content declaration, DC approval; **examiner panels** (≥ 2 external, one outside state/country), invitations, report tracking with recommendations and remuneration; **viva-voce** scheduling/panel/result (open defence); **award** → Phase 18 `degrees_awarded(award_type='phd')` + provisional certificate + fellowship stop; **ETD upload** (Shodhganga/institutional) within 30 days; scholar portal — with self (scholar) vs guide (own scholars) vs research cell/dean (all) views and NIRF GPHD/NAAC metrics.

## 31.6 Services
- `scholar_service.py` (`phd.core`)
  1. Create on admission (Phase 08 admits into a `level='phd'` programme → event `student.admitted` → scholar shell) or manually; guide allocation with load check (`current < max_scholars` by cadre rule from `phd.rules` overriding `faculty_profiles.max_scholars` when set) under `FOR UPDATE`; co-guide (internal/external); mode; fellowship type; `registration_date` set when coursework completes (`registered` status) and `max_duration_end = registration_date + max_duration_months` (relaxations by category/gender/PwD); timeline of statuses; registration letter (Phase 26).
  2. Status machine: `admitted → coursework → registered → synopsis_submitted → thesis_submitted → under_evaluation → viva_scheduled → awarded`; `discontinued` from any pre-award state (with reason: unsatisfactory progress ×2, max duration exceeded without extension, voluntary); duration warnings at max−6 months; min duration enforced before synopsis (`min_duration_months` from registration).
  3. Guide change/mode change/leave/extension/exemption through requests (below); guide load dashboard; scholar 360 (coursework, DC, reports, fellowship, publications tagged in Phase 30, ethics approval link).
- `coursework_service.py` (`phd.coursework`): plan courses (research methodology mandatory + electives) → registration via Phase 09 (`registration_type='regular'` in the PhD programme term) → results via Phase 15; `credits_earned` and `methodology_done` computed from `student_course_results`; deadline (12 months) with warnings; exemption request (M.Phil/prior coursework) → `exempted`; completion → `registered` (+ `registration_date`).
- `dc_service.py` (`phd.dc_meetings`): constitute DC (guide as convenor, ≥ 1 external per policy, subject experts) — optional link to Phase 33 committee; meetings (`meeting_no` unique per scholar) with agenda, attendance, minutes, recommendations (action items with due dates) → `held → approved` (convenor); pre-synopsis presentations recorded as DC meetings; DC approval of thesis via workflow `phd.thesis_dc_approval`.
- `progress_service.py` (`phd.progress_reports`): half-yearly periods (`period_label` e.g. `2026-H1`) generated per scholar from registration; scholar submits (report + summary) → guide review → DC rating at the next meeting (`satisfactory|unsatisfactory`); reminders before due; **two consecutive `unsatisfactory` → `phd.cancellation_review` flag** → research cell/dean decision (discontinue or warning) via `phd.request`-style workflow instance; report compliance dashboard.
- `fellowship_service.py` (`phd.fellowships`)
  1. Fellowship record (type, monthly amount from defaults, HRA rule, contingency yearly, funding source: UGC/CSIR/institution/project → Phase 23 ledger/grant head); status `active|held|closed`.
  2. Monthly disbursement run (job): eligible = active fellowship, scholar status not `discontinued`, progress report not overdue beyond grace, attendance/leave rules (part-time none), max duration not exceeded → `fellowship_disbursements(month, amount)` `pending → approved` (research cell) → payment via Phase 06 payroll batch (`payroll_ref`) or Phase 23 payout (bank transfer batch, `expense_records(source_type='fellowship')`) → `paid`; holds with reason (unsatisfactory report, leave without pay), arrears on release; contingency claims (bills) as separate disbursements; JRF → SRF upgrade after 2 years with DC recommendation (creates new fellowship row); certificates/statements for agencies (UGC/CSIR formats).
- `request_service.py` (`phd.extensions_guide_change`): `extension` (months ≤ policy max, with justification and DC recommendation) → on approve extends `max_duration_end`; `change_of_guide` (new guide load check; old guide NOC step) → reassign + DC reconstitution prompt; `mode_change` (full ↔ part-time; fellowship implications); `leave` (maternity/medical/other; fellowship hold rules); `coursework_exemption`; all via workflow `phd.request` (guide → research cell → dean) with type-specific extra steps; decisions update the scholar and notify.
- `thesis_service.py` (`phd.thesis_plagiarism`)
  1. Stages: `pre_synopsis` (DC presentation), `synopsis` (after min duration, coursework done, ≥ N publications/presentations per policy `pre_submission_requirements` e.g. 1 paper in a refereed journal (Phase 30 verified publication tagged to the scholar) + 2 conference presentations), `thesis` (within 6 months of synopsis per setting), `revised_thesis` (after `major`).
  2. Plagiarism: on submit → `PlagiarismProvider.submit(file)` (mock returns deterministic %), callback stores `similarity_pct`, report URL, `ai_generated_pct` when supported; **`similarity_pct ≤ max`** (excluding references/quotes per adapter config) required for `plagiarism_checked → dc_approved`; AI declaration mandatory; re-submission after revision; certificate of plagiarism check (Phase 26 template).
  3. DC approval workflow → `dc_approved`; scholar status transitions (`synopsis_submitted`, `thesis_submitted`); synopsis acknowledgement letter; fee for thesis submission via `fee_service.create_invoice(source_type='thesis_submission')` when configured.
- `examiner_service.py` (`phd.external_examiners`): guide proposes a panel (≥ `examiners_min_external`, ≥ 1 outside state/country, conflict-of-interest declaration, no co-authors of the scholar in the last 3 years — checked against Phase 30 `publications.authors`) → workflow `phd.examiner_panel` (dean/VC approve; sealed panel — scholar cannot see) → research cell invites (email with tokenised accept/decline link; external examiner account via Phase 02 external-examiner role when they need the portal) → `accepted` → thesis dispatched (secure signed link, watermarked) → report upload (recommendation `accept|minor|major|reject`, remarks) with due-date reminders and replacement flow on decline/no response → remuneration line (Phase 06 external remuneration or Phase 23 payout); `under_evaluation` while awaiting reports; consolidated recommendation: all `accept|minor` → viva eligible; any `major` → revised thesis cycle (back to `thesis` stage with `revised_thesis` submission and re-examination by the same examiner); `reject` → research board decision (discontinue/re-submission after 1 year).
- `viva_service.py` (`phd.viva`): schedule (date, venue/link, panel = guide + external examiner(s) + DC per policy, open defence notice published to calendar/website), attendance, result `recommended|re_viva|not_recommended` with report; `re_viva` reschedules once; `recommended` → award request.
- `award_service.py`: workflow `phd.award` (research cell → dean → academic council resolution when `governance.minutes`) → Phase 18 `degrees_awarded(award_type='phd', approval_resolution_id)` → provisional PhD certificate (Phase 26) → `awarded`; fellowship closed; ETD deadline started; notification to scholar/guide; UGC "PhD awarded" return rows (Phase 36 statutory).
- `etd_service.py` (`phd.etd_upload`): package thesis + metadata (Dublin Core: title, author, guide, department, year, keywords, abstract, embargo) → `EtdRepository.upload` (Shodhganga via INFLIBNET adapter — mock returns a handle; institutional DSpace via Phase 19 adapter) → `handle`, `uploaded_at`; within 30 days of award (reminders); embargo requests (patent-related) with approval; status dashboard.
- `stats_service.py`: scholars by status/guide/department, average time to award, awarded per year (NIRF **GPHD**), guide load utilisation, fellowship spend, plagiarism stats, examiner turnaround; metrics to Phase 32 (`nirf.gphd`, `faculty.phd_guides`, `research.phd_awarded`) and Phase 35 dashboards; UGC/AISHE PhD returns to Phase 36.
