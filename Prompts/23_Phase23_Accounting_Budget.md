# PHASE 23 — ACCOUNTING & BUDGET

## Pre-Requisite
Phases 01–12 complete (fee heads with `ledger_account_id`, invoices/payments/refunds/day-book, minimal `ledger_accounts` chart from Phase 12, workflow engine, background jobs). Phase 22 (vendor bills, budget commitments, depreciation) and Phase 30 (research projects) integrate through events when present.

## Objective
Institution-level finance on top of the fee module: a **chart of accounts** (income/expense/asset/liability/equity, hierarchical), automatic **ledger postings** from fee collections/refunds, vendor bill payments, payroll runs, hostel/mess/transport invoices and asset depreciation; manual **income and expense records** with approval workflow; **budgets** per year × college × department with allocation, commitment and utilisation tracking and over-budget controls; **grant ledgers** for funded projects with utilisation certificates; bank reconciliation against uploaded statements; GST reports (output tax on taxable fee heads, input on bills); **Tally/Zoho/CSV exports** of vouchers; financial statements (receipts & payments, income & expenditure, balance summary) and audit-ready registers — with department (own budget/expenses) vs accounts (all) views.

## Feature keys
`accounting.core` (off, standard); `accounting.budgets` (off, standard; depends `accounting.core`); `accounting.grants_ledger` (off, professional); `accounting.gst_reports` (off, standard); `accounting.tally_export` (off, standard); `fees.accounting_export` (Phase 12) delegates here.

## 23.6 Services
- `chart_service.py` (`accounting.core`): accounts tree (code uniqueness per institution, type inherited from parent unless overridden), activate/deactivate (blocked when postings exist), `external_ref` mapping for Tally/Zoho ledger names, import from pack template or CSV, mapping UI for `fee_heads.ledger_account_id`, payroll components and inventory categories → accounts (stored in `institution_settings['accounting.mappings']`).
- `posting_service.py` — **single write path for automatic entries** (idempotent per `(source_type, source_id, event)`):
  1. `fees.payment_received` → `income_records(ledger = fee head account, source_type='fee_payment')` split per invoice item head; caution deposit heads post to the liability account; advance/pre-term payments to "fees received in advance" when the invoice term is future (setting).
  2. `fees.receipt_cancelled/bounced` → reversing income record (negative amount, `source_type='fee_payment_reversal'`); `fees.refund_processed` → expense record (refund account) or liability release for caution deposits.
  3. `inventory.bill_paid` → expense record (mapped category account) with TDS/GST payable splits; `payroll.paid` (Phase 06 run marked paid) → salary expense + PF/ESI/TDS payables; `inventory.asset_depreciated` → depreciation expense; `inventory.asset_disposed` → income (sale) and asset write-down; `alumni.donation_received` (Phase 29) → donation income (restricted fund when campaign says so).
  4. Replays are safe (unique key); postings carry `college_id`/`department_id` from the source for segment reporting; audit row per posting.
- `records_service.py`: manual income (donations in kind, interest, rentals, misc) and expenses (petty cash, travel, events, utilities): expense → workflow `accounting.expense` (accountant → principal above threshold; HOD-recorded department expenses start at accountant), approved → `paid` with reference; attachments required above `document_required_paise`; budget linkage (below); GST input capture on expenses when `accounting.gst_reports`.
- `budget_service.py` (`accounting.budgets`)
  1. Heads per year × college × department × name (UNIQUE) with `allocated_paise`; allocation workflow `accounting.budget_allocation`; status `draft → approved → frozen (year closed)`; revisions and transfers between heads (audited, both under `FOR UPDATE`).
  2. Commitments: `budget.commit_requested` (indent approved, PO created, event proposal approved, scholarship sanctioned) → `committed_paise += amount`; `budget.commit_released` on cancel or when converted to utilisation.
  3. Utilisation: approved/paid expense records with `budget_head_id` → `utilised_paise += amount` (and commitment released when the expense references the committing document).
  4. Controls: `available = allocated − utilised − committed`; hard stop (409 `BUDGET_EXCEEDED`) when `accounting.budget_hard_stop` else warning; threshold notifications at 80 % and 100 % to HOD/accountant; year-end carry-forward rule (`none|unspent`), utilisation report per department for NAAC 6.4.
- `grant_service.py` (`accounting.grants_ledger`): ledgers per funded project (from Phase 30 `research.project_sanctioned` event or manual) with agency budget heads (manpower, equipment, consumables, travel, contingency, overheads), receipts (instalments), expenses per head (PI records via Phase 30 purchase/expense requests or direct), head-wise available with reallocation (agency-rule flags), **utilisation certificate** PDF (GFR 12-A style for IN pack) and statement of expenditure per period, closure with unspent refund; interest earned tracking; audit trail.
- `reconciliation_service.py`: upload bank statement (CSV/XLSX, mapped columns) per bank account and date → auto-match rows to `fee_payments` (by UTR/reference/amount/date ± 2 days), expense payments and income records; unmatched items list with manual matching, bank charges/interest as new records; `difference_paise` recomputed; reconciled when difference = 0 (or explained items); monthly reminder when open.
- `gst_service.py` (`accounting.gst_reports`): output tax from taxable fee invoice items (Phase 12 GST fields) and vendor-store sales; input tax from vendor bills; GSTR-1/3B-style summaries per period; HSN/SAC mapping per fee head; export CSV.
- `export_service.py` (`accounting.tally_export`): voucher export jobs (receipts, payments, journals, sales/purchase) for a period to Tally XML (ledger names via `external_ref`), Zoho Books CSV, or generic CSV; marks exported rows (`accounting_exports.id` on records via settings map) to avoid double export; download via signed URL; Phase 12 `fees.accounting_export` endpoint redirects here.
- `statements_service.py`: receipts & payments (cash basis), income & expenditure (accrual-lite from records), balance summary (cash/bank/receivables/deposits/payables), trial balance by account, segment views (college/department), period close (lock records before date; only reversals after) and financial-year close; exports PDF/XLSX; NAAC 6.4 and NIRF financial metrics to Phase 32 (`capex_paise`, `opex_paise`, `library_spend`, `research_spend`).
