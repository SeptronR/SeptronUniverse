# PHASE 27 — MENTORING, GRIEVANCES, DISCIPLINE, SURVEYS & CLUBS

## Pre-Requisite
Phases 01–13 complete (students/guardians, staff, batches/sections with class advisors, attendance summaries, internal marks, fees/dues, holds, workflow engine with `grievance.escalation`, communication core, OBE definitions for survey CO/PO tags). Phase 25 (counselling appointment slots, events) and Phase 33 (committees UI) enrich but are not required — a minimal `committees` CRUD stub (`governance:committees`) ships with this phase and is replaced by Phase 33.

## Objective
Student support and voice: **mentoring** (assignments per term with bulk/rule-based allocation, bookable appointment slots incl. guardians, session logs with confidential notes and action items, follow-ups, referrals to counselling), an **early-warning risk score** per student per term (attendance, CIA, dues, standing, hostel, engagement) with mentor/HOD alerts; **grievances** (authenticated or anonymous, category → committee routing (grievance cell / ICC / anti-ragging / SC-ST / OBC cells), SLA, updates, escalation workflow, satisfaction rating, statutory registers); **discipline** (cases for students and staff, inquiry → hearing → decision with sanctions incl. fines/suspension/expulsion effects, appeals, register); **surveys** (course/faculty/institution/course-exit/programme-exit/alumni/employer/hostel/parent/**SSS**, anonymous with respondent hashing, analytics per target, OBE indirect attainment feed, NAAC 2.7.1); **clubs & activities** (clubs/NSS/NCC/chapters, members/office bearers, club events linked to Phase 25 events, **activity-hours ledger** with verification and credit mapping, verified achievements) — with self (my mentor/mentees, my grievances, my surveys, my clubs) vs officer (all) views.

## Feature keys
`mentoring.core`, `mentoring.appointment_slots`, `mentoring.counselling` (on, standard); `mentoring.early_warning` (off, professional; depends `analytics.early_warning` — the scoring job lives here, Phase 35 adds dashboards/read models); `grievances.core`, `grievances.anonymous`, `grievances.committees` (on, standard); `discipline.core` (on, standard; depends `governance.committees`); `surveys.core`, `surveys.sss` (on, standard); `clubs.core`, `clubs.achievements` (on, standard); `clubs.activity_hours_credits` (off, standard).

## 27.6 Services
- `mentoring_service.py` (`mentoring.core`, `mentoring.appointment_slots`, `mentoring.counselling`)
  1. Assignments per term: bulk by rule (`section_advisor` → class advisor mentors the section; `round_robin` across department faculty by ratio; `department` manual), carry-forward from previous term (setting), reassignment with history; one active per enrolment per term (UNIQUE); mentor load report vs `default_ratio`.
  2. Mentee 360° (`students:view` via ownership): attendance %, CIA summary, dues, holds, standing, hostel status, risk, sessions, referrals, achievements — read through the other modules' services with the mentor principal (visibility enforced by ownership).
  3. Slots (`mentoring.appointment_slots`): mentor publishes slots (recurring), students/guardians book (capacity under `FOR UPDATE`), reminders, no-show marking; a booking may create a session record.
  4. Sessions: notes, `concerns` (academic/personal/financial/health/career), action items with owners/due dates and follow-up reminders (Beat), `is_confidential` (visible only to the author mentor and counsellors; HOD/IQAC see only that a session occurred), `share_with_guardian` → guardian sees a summary; minimum sessions per term compliance report (NAAC 5.x mentor-mentee ratio evidence).
  5. Referrals (`mentoring.counselling`): mentor/advisor refers to counselling (`counselling_referrals`), counsellor accepts → books a Phase 25 appointment slot with the student's consent (event), status mirror; strictly confidential; aggregates only.
- `risk_service.py` (`mentoring.early_warning`): `compute(term, scope)` job → per enrolment score = Σ weights × normalised factor: attendance shortfall (from `attendance_summaries` avg % vs 75), CIA (below pass % components count), dues (overdue amount bands), standing (`probation|warning`), hostel (late returns/absences), engagement (LMS content views, assignment lateness when Phase 13 data exists); `level` by thresholds; upsert per term (UNIQUE); deltas emit `risk.flag_raised` (new `high`) to mentor + HOD; acknowledgement by mentor with action; consumers: Phase 11 `attendance.below_threshold` triggers a recompute for that student; Phase 35 reads flags for dashboards; students see only their level when `risk.show_to_student`.
- `grievance_service.py` (`grievances.core`, `grievances.anonymous`, `grievances.committees`)
  1. Create (auth or anonymous public form with captcha; anonymous → `raised_by_user_id NULL`, `is_anonymous`, tracking `ticket_number` + secret shown once); category → `committee` per routing (`grievances.committees`; ICC for harassment with POSH timelines; anti-ragging with UGC mandatory escalation to the anti-ragging squad within 24 h), SLA from `sla_hours`; `student_program_enrollment_id` optional (on behalf by parent).
  2. Triage (grievance officer or committee secretary): assign, priority, committee change (audited); updates (internal notes hidden from the complainant), attachments; status machine `open → acknowledged → under_review → resolved → closed` (auto-close after 7 days unless reopened); `escalated` via workflow `grievance.escalation` (grievance_officer → principal) on SLA breach (Beat) or manual; reopen once by complainant; rating on close.
  3. Confidential categories (harassment/ragging/discrimination): visible only to the routed committee members + grievance officer; department scope excluded; audit on every read; anonymised statutory registers (UGC grievance redressal, ICC annual report, anti-ragging compliance) and NAAC 7.x/`governance.grievance_sla_pct` metric.
  4. Complainant view: timeline (non-internal), status, SLA, rating; anonymous tracking by ticket + secret; notifications via chosen channel (email/in-app; none for anonymous unless a contact is given).
- `discipline_service.py` (`discipline.core`)
  1. Cases for students or staff from reports (manual, hostel discipline events, exam malpractice referrals from Phase 16, false SOS from Phase 25, placement policy violations from Phase 28 — via `source`); `case_number` from sequence; evidence; inquiry (committee assignment, notice to subject + guardian (student), statement submission via portal, hearing scheduling with attendance); decision via workflow `discipline.case` (committee chair → principal for `suspension|expulsion`) with sanctions: `warning` (record), `fine` (invoice `source_type='discipline_fine'` via fee linkage), `suspension` (dates → attendance `exempt`/`absent` policy, hold `all` for the period, LMS access flag), `community_service` (hours target tracked via `activity_hours(activity_type='community_service')`), `hostel_eviction` (event → Phase 20), `debar_placement` (event → Phase 28 blacklist), `expulsion` (Phase 07 status `expelled` + `all` hold; staff → HR event `hr.disciplinary_action`); decision letter (Phase 26 template).
  2. Appeal within window → `discipline.appeal` workflow → uphold/modify/revoke sanctions with reversal effects (fine cancel, hold release); `closed`; register export (statutory) and counts to Phase 32.
- `survey_service.py` (`surveys.core`, `surveys.sss`)
  1. Surveys by type with question templates (course feedback with CO tags, faculty feedback, SSS (NAAC template 21 questions, 5-point), programme exit with PO tags, alumni/employer (external respondents via tokenised links), hostel, parent); targets auto-derived (course/faculty → each offering the respondent is registered in; hostel → residents); audience rules; open/close windows; reminders (Beat) to non-respondents computed from expected respondents minus `respondent_hash` set — **the hash (HMAC(user_id, survey_id, secret)) never maps back**; one response per target per respondent (UNIQUE).
  2. Analytics per target: n, per-question mean/distribution, overall score, masked comments (PII scrub), comparisons (department/institution), trend vs previous term; faculty see own offerings only after `min_responses` (setting 5) to protect anonymity; CO/PO indirect scores exported to Phase 17 (`obe.indirect_survey_scores` event with `survey_id`, target, outcome → score); SSS score → `surveys.sss_score` metric (Phase 32) with the NAAC computation (weighted average of 20 quantitative items / max).
  3. Action taken on feedback: HOD/IQAC record actions per target (stored in `feedback_surveys`? no — in `cqi_actions` Phase 17 when OBE enabled, else a note on the analytics row via `institution_settings['surveys.actions']`); publish summary to students (setting).
- `club_service.py` (`clubs.core`, `clubs.activity_hours_credits`, `clubs.achievements`)
  1. Clubs with type, advisor, budget, members with roles (office bearers per year), join requests (approval by advisor), club events (linked to Phase 25 `events` for venue/budget/certificates; report/images for NAAC 5.3.2/3.6.x).
  2. Activity hours ledger: entries by student (self with evidence) or officer (bulk from club events/sports participation/NSS camps); verification (`clubs:verify_hours`: NSS/NCC officer, club advisor, sports officer); `credits_course_id` mapping when hours ≥ `min_hours` for a mandatory non-credit/value-added course (`clubs.activity_hours_credits`) → event `activity.credit_eligible` → Phase 15 result row (`result_status='pass'`, `counts_toward_cgpa=false`) through the internal marks/result path; community-service sanctions consume the same ledger.
  3. Achievements: student submits with evidence; verification by officer/advisor; verified achievements feed Phase 28 profile, Phase 32 (5.3.1 awards), Phase 26 certificates; sports participation (Phase 25) creates achievement rows automatically.
- `committee_stub_service.py`: minimal `committees`/`committee_members` CRUD for grievance/discipline committees (`governance:committees`) until Phase 33 replaces it (same tables, no data migration).
- `stats_service.py`: mentoring coverage and session compliance, risk distribution, grievance SLA/category stats, discipline register, survey completion/scores, club participation, achievements — to Phase 32/35.
