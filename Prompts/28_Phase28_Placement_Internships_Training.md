# PHASE 28 — PLACEMENT, INTERNSHIPS & TRAINING

## Pre-Requisite
Phases 01–16 complete (students with applicant academic history from Phase 08, current CGPA/backlogs from published results, attendance %, holds, communication core, calendar/events from Phase 25 for drive scheduling, documents from Phase 26 for NOC letters and offer archiving). Phase 27 feeds verified achievements and discipline `debar_placement` sanctions.

## Objective
Training & placement for a college/university: company master with tiers and recruiter users, **placement policies** per batch/programme (max offers, dream-offer upgrade, opt-out), **drives** with rule-based eligibility evaluated live (CGPA, active/history backlogs, 10th/12th %, gap years, programmes/batches, already-placed rule) and snapshotted at application, overrides with reason, publication with eligible-list notification, rounds with bulk results and funnel, **offers** (accept/decline/PPO/joining tracking) enforcing policy, student placement profiles (resume builder, skills, opt-out, higher studies), **blacklisting** for policy violations, **recruiter portal** (post drives, view consented shortlist fields, schedule rounds, upload results, feedback), **internships** (records, NOC letters, evaluation, credits mapping), **training programmes** (aptitude/soft-skills/technical/mock interviews with attendance and scores), and statistics in NIRF/NAAC formats — with self (my profile/drives/offers) vs placement officer (all) views.

## Feature keys
`placement.core`, `placement.internships`, `placement.training`, `placement.resume_builder` (on, standard); `placement.recruiter_portal` (off, professional; depends `placement.core`); `portals.recruiter` (Phase 39 shell) uses this phase's endpoints.

## 28.6 Services
- `company_service.py`: companies with tier, contacts, MoU link (Phase 30 `mous` when placement-type), blacklist companies (no new drives), recruiter invitations (`placement.recruiter_portal`): email invite → account with `recruiter` role bound to `companies.recruiter_user_ids`; company page with history (drives, hires, feedback average).
- `policy_service.py`: policies per batch/programme (fallback defaults): `max_offers` (1 = one-offer policy), `dream_ctc_threshold_paise` with `allow_upgrade_if_dream` (a placed student may apply only to drives with `ctc_min ≥ threshold` or `tier in (dream, super_dream)`), `opt_out_allowed`, `rules` JSONB (e.g. `internship_ppo_counts_as_offer`, `mass_recruiter_cap`); evaluated by the eligibility engine.
- `eligibility_service.py`
  1. `evaluate(drive, enrolment)` live: CGPA from current `student_term_results` (latest published, `is_current`), active backlogs from `student_arrears(open)`, history backlogs (ever failed count), 10th/12th % from `applicants.qualifying_exams` (or profile-entered when no applicant record; flagged unverified), gap years from admission vs 12th year, programme/batch filters, attendance % (optional), `placed_status` vs policy (already placed → only dream upgrade), holds (`placement`? use `all`), blacklist, opt-out, discipline `debar_placement` sanction; returns `is_eligible` + `reasons[]`; preview counts for a drive before publishing.
  2. Snapshot on apply (`eligibility_snapshot`) — later CGPA changes do not retro-affect; override (`placement:override_eligibility`) with reason, audited; re-evaluation endpoint when results are republished (setting).
- `drive_service.py` (`placement.core`)
  1. Drive lifecycle `draft → open (publish) → closed (deadline) → in_progress (rounds) → completed | cancelled`; publish computes the eligible list and notifies eligible students (`placement.drive_published`) + calendar entries (Phase 25) for `drive_on`/rounds; registration deadline enforced; `pooled` drives across colleges of a university tenant.
  2. Applications: student applies (eligibility check, one per drive, `Idempotency-Key`), withdraw before shortlist; resume snapshot (`resume_url` at apply); shortlist/upload by officer or recruiter (bulk by registration numbers with partial-success report); rounds (`round_no`, type, schedule/venue) with per-round results bulk (`cleared|not_cleared|absent`), `absent` after shortlist counts toward blacklist rules; funnel per round; `selected` at final round.
  3. Offers: create from `selected` applications (or PPO from internship) → `offered`; **policy enforcement on accept** under `FOR UPDATE`: `max_offers` (accepting a second offer → 409 unless dream upgrade: previous accepted offer auto-`declined` with reason `dream_upgrade` when allowed), `placement_status` → `placed|multiple`; decline reason; `joined|not_joined` updates after joining date (Beat reminders to officer); revocation by company; offer letter archived via Phase 26 (`issued_documents(entity_type='placement_offer')`, external doc kept as attachment).
  4. Blacklist (`student_placement_profiles.is_blacklisted`) manual with reason/until or automatic by rules (no-show after shortlist ≥ N, decline after accept) → `placement.policy_violation` event (Phase 27 discipline case optional), notification; unblacklist audited.
  5. Company feedback per drive (ratings on candidates/process) → company/institution reports.
- `profile_service.py` (`placement.resume_builder`): profile with academics auto-filled (read-only: CGPA, backlogs, attendance, achievements verified in Phase 27), skills, projects/internships pulled from Phase 13/28, resume builder (`resume_json` sections → PDF via Phase 26 template `resume_*` layouts; multiple versions), opt-out with reason (higher studies details captured for NAAC 5.2.2 / NIRF GUE), `consent_share_profile` (DPDP — required for recruiter visibility), profile completeness score, officer bulk export (masked for auditors).
- `internship_service.py` (`placement.internships`): records (company from master or free text), duration ≥ `internship_min_weeks` for credit mapping, **NOC letter** request → Phase 26 template `internship_noc` → `noc_document_id`; mentor evaluation (rubric, marks) → when `credits_course_id` set → marks flow to Phase 13 internal marks / Phase 15 result for the internship course (single write path); certificate upload; summer/semester-long internship windows from calendar; NAAC 1.3.4 metric `academics.internships_pct`.
- `training_service.py` (`placement.training`): programmes (type, trainer/vendor, batches, sessions), attendance bulk (QR optional via Phase 11 QR utility), scores (aptitude/mock interview), readiness score per student (aggregated into profile), vendor costs → Phase 23 expense (optional); reminders.
- `recruiter_service.py` (`placement.recruiter_portal`): recruiter scoped to own company: create/edit drives (`draft`; officer publishes), view eligible/applicant list with **consented fields only** (`placement.consented_fields` ∩ student consent; no phone/email unless consented), schedule rounds, upload round results, download shortlist (watermarked, audited), submit offers (officer confirms), feedback; all actions audited with company id.
- `stats_service.py`: `placed_pct = placed / (eligible − opted_out)` (NAAC 5.2.1; eligible = final-year students meeting policy), median/average/highest CTC (NIRF GMS on `nirf_median_basis`), higher studies % (5.2.2), by programme/company/tier, PPO counts, offers per student, drives per company, funnel conversion; NIRF placement data sheet export; publishes `placement.placed_pct`, `nirf.gms`, `students.higher_studies_pct`, `academics.internships_pct` metrics to Phase 32.
